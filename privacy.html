Privacy policy: job-agent

job-agent is a personal tool used only by its developer.

  • It requests read-only access to the developer's own Gmail account (the Google scope gmail.readonly) in order to find job-alert and newsletter emails.
  • Email content is processed on the developer's own computer.
  • Nothing is shared with third parties, sold, or used for advertising.
  • No other person's data is collected, and the application has no other users.
  • Access can be revoked at any time in the Google Account settings under "Third-party apps with account access".
